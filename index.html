Research Profile

Ph.D. research scholar in Chemistry at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore, based in the Smart Materials Laboratory for Biosensing and Catalysis. Current doctoral research focuses on fluorescence-based quantum dot sensors for biologically important analytes, working toward affordable optical and electrochemical sensing platforms for healthcare diagnostics accessible to all sections of society. Combines hands-on nanomaterial synthesis and spectroscopic characterization with computational (DFT) methods, and is an active member of the Indian Photobiology Society and the Network of Researchers on the Chemical Emergence of Life (NoRCEL).
